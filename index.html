04

Warehouse Inventory Optimization Dashboard

A mid-sized logistics company was experiencing 23% excess stock in slow-moving SKUs while simultaneously running out of high-demand items — costing the business in storage fees and lost sales.

Impact

Reorder point optimization reduced stockouts by 31%, excess inventory costs dropped by 18%, and warehouse space utilisation improved from 61% to 79% within one quarter.

Key Findings

  • Top 20% of SKUs accounted for 74% of stock movement (Pareto analysis)
  • Average lead time variance of 6.2 days was the primary cause of stockouts
  • Dead stock identified across 112 SKUs — flagged for clearance strategy
  • Safety stock levels recalculated using demand variability & supplier reliability scores
